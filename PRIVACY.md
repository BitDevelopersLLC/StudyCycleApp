# Privacy Policy

**Study Cycle collects no data. None.**

_Last updated: November 12, 2026_

<!-- Keep in sync with website/privacy.html. -->

## What we collect

Nothing. Study Cycle has:

- no account or sign-in,
- no analytics, tracking or advertising,
- no crash reporting,
- no server. The app never connects to the internet.

Your settings (cycle lengths and timer options), the current timer and your study totals (focus
minutes and finished cycles per day) are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes
them, and you can delete your study totals at any time on the Progress screen. On iPhone, they may
be included in your own device backup if you have turned that on. On Android, Study Cycle turns off
both cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer, so they stay on this phone.

## Notifications

Study Cycle shows a notification when a focus block or a break ends. These notifications are
scheduled on your phone by its own alarm system. No server sends them, and no notification
token or identifier is created.

## Android permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | To tell you when a focus block or break ends. Android asks you first. |
| `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` | So those alerts arrive on time while the phone is idle. On Android 14 and later you choose whether to allow it ("Alarms & reminders"). |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | To restore the alerts if your phone restarts during a study session. |
| `VIBRATE` | To vibrate with the alert. |

That's the complete list. The app does **not** have the `INTERNET` permission, so it is
technically unable to send data anywhere.

## iPhone and iPad permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | To tell you when a focus block or break ends. iOS asks you first. |

## This website

studycycle.app uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other websites.

Like every website, our hosting provider's servers automatically record standard technical logs
(IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time). These logs are used only to keep the
site running and secure. We don't use them to identify you or track you.

## If you email us

If you write to support@studycycle.app, we receive your email address and message. We use them
only to reply to you. We never add you to a mailing list or share your address. Ask us anytime
and we'll delete your messages.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Privacy Policy

Study Cycle collects no data. None.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Study Cycle has:

  • no account or sign-in,
  • no analytics, tracking or advertising,
  • no crash reporting,
  • no server. The app never connects to the internet.

Your settings (cycle lengths and timer options), the current timer and your study totals (focus minutes and finished cycles per day) are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them, and you can delete your study totals at any time on the Progress screen. On iPhone, they may be included in your own device backup if you have turned that on. Android app backup is turned off for Study Cycle.

Notifications

Study Cycle shows a notification when a focus block or a break ends. These notifications are scheduled on your phone by its own alarm system. No server sends them, and no notification token or identifier is created.

Android permissions

PermissionWhy
POST_NOTIFICATIONSTo tell you when a focus block or break ends. Android asks you first.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARMSo those alerts arrive on time while the phone is idle. On Android 14 and later you choose whether to allow it ("Alarms & reminders").
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDTo restore the alerts if your phone restarts during a study session.
VIBRATETo vibrate with the alert.

That's the complete list. The app does not have the INTERNET permission, so it is technically unable to send data anywhere.

iPhone and iPad permissions

NotificationsTo tell you when a focus block or break ends. iOS asks you first.

This website

studycycle.app uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other websites.

Like every website, our hosting provider's servers automatically record standard technical logs (IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time). These logs are used only to keep the site running and secure. We don't use them to identify you or track you.

If you email us

If you write to support@studycycle.app, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to reply to you. We never add you to a mailing list or share your address. Ask us anytime and we'll delete your messages.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here and in the app's source code repository.
